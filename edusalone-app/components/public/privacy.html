EduSalone Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 May 2026  ·  Version 1.0

This is a plain-language privacy policy for EduSalone. It explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we protect it, and your rights. We wrote this so any parent, principal, or teacher can understand it — not just lawyers.

1. Who we are

EduSalone is a school management platform owned and operated by PalmTech Group Ltd., a company registered in Sierra Leone. Throughout this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to PalmTech Group Ltd.

2. What data we collect

We collect different data depending on who you are:

Schools (Principal accounts)

  • School name, address, and contact details
  • Principal’s name, email, and phone number
  • School logo and motto (optional)
  • Subscription and payment history

Teachers

  • Full name
  • Email and phone number
  • Subjects assigned and classes taught
  • Account login activity

Students

  • Full name and admission number
  • Class, sex, and date of birth
  • Academic grades and attendance records
  • Affective traits and psychomotor skills assessments
  • Teacher and principal comments

Parents / Guardians

  • Full name and contact details (phone, email)
  • Relationship to student
  • Fee payment records

Technical data (everyone)

  • Device type and operating system
  • IP address (for security and fraud prevention)
  • Login times and session duration
  • Crash logs and error reports

3. How we use your data

We only use data for purposes that directly benefit users:

  • Operating the service — login, dashboards, grade entry, attendance tracking, fee management, PDF report card generation
  • Communicating with users — sending receipts, account notifications, and (with permission) updates about new features
  • Improving the service — analysing crash reports and usage patterns to fix bugs and improve features
  • Security — detecting fraud, blocking unauthorised access, and protecting accounts
  • Legal compliance — meeting our obligations under Sierra Leone law and applicable international laws (such as GDPR for European users)

4. How we protect children’s data

EduSalone processes data about children. We take this seriously.

Children’s data is only added to EduSalone by their school’s Principal or assigned teachers — never directly by the child. Schools are required to obtain parental consent before adding student data, in line with their own enrolment policies. We do not sell, rent, or share children’s data with anyone outside the school’s approved staff and parents.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your data. Ever. We only share data with the following parties, and only when necessary to operate the service:

  • Within your school community — Principals can see all students and teachers in their school. Teachers can see students in their classes. Parents can see only their own children’s records.
  • Service providers we depend on — Supabase (database hosting), Netlify (web hosting), and Google (Android delivery via Play Store). Each is contractually required to protect your data.
  • Legal requirements — if compelled by Sierra Leone or applicable international law, we may disclose data to government authorities. We will notify you if this happens, unless legally prohibited.
  • Business transfers — if PalmTech is acquired, merged, or sold, your data transfers with the company. The new owner must honour this privacy policy or notify you of changes.

6. Where your data is stored

EduSalone data is stored on secure cloud servers operated by Supabase, primarily in European data centres. By using EduSalone, you understand and agree that your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside Sierra Leone. We ensure all such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Active accounts: data retained for as long as the account is active.
  • Suspended accounts: data retained for 12 months in case the school renews subscription. After 12 months, archived or deleted.
  • Closed accounts: personal data deleted within 90 days of closure. Anonymous statistical data may be retained for analytics.
  • Financial records: retained for 7 years to meet Sierra Leone tax law.

8. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate data
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format (CSV/Excel)
  • Objection — object to certain types of processing
  • Withdrawal of consent — withdraw consent at any time where we relied on it
  • Complaint — file a complaint with the Sierra Leone Data Protection Commission or your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at mmans.sl.001@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy Request.” We will respond within 30 days.

9. Security measures

We protect your data with industry-standard security:

  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt (never stored in plain text)
  • All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • JWT-based authentication tokens with 7-day expiry
  • Multi-tenant database with strict row-level access controls
  • Automated daily backups
  • Regular security audits and code reviews

If a security breach affects your data, we will notify you within 72 hours of discovering it, in line with international best practice. We will also report to the Sierra Leone Data Protection Commission as required by law.

10. Cookies and tracking

EduSalone uses essential cookies to keep you logged in and to remember your preferences. We do not use advertising cookies. We do not track you across other websites. We do not sell your browsing data.

11. Third-party links

EduSalone may contain links to third-party services (e.g. Orange Money, Afrimoney for future payment integration). When you click these links, you leave EduSalone. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. Please review their privacy policies separately.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services evolve or as laws change. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Post the updated policy at edusalone.netlify.app/privacy
  • Notify users via email or in-app notification at least 30 days before changes take effect
  • Update the version number and effective date at the top of this document

13. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of Sierra Leone. Any disputes arising from this policy will be resolved in Sierra Leone courts. For users in the European Union, additional rights under GDPR also apply.
